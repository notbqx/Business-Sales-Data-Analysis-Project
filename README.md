# Business-Sales-Data-Analysis-Project
A department store chain wants to know how to maximize profits. Used Excel and PowerBI to solve the business problem using data

●	Built an interactive Power BI dashboard that visualized a department store’s sales data to find out how to maximize profits.

●	Cleaned messy data and corrected outliers to prepare data for analysis using Excel.

My recommendation:

To maximize overall profitability, the business should prioritize acquiring Small Business accounts and cross-selling Office Supplies, which yields our highest profit margin at 71.85%. Additionally, operations must scale up inventory allocations for Electronics and Home Goods starting in Q3 to fully capture the ~60% demand spike seen during Q4 holiday sales.
